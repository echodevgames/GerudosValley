Our Promise

The gates of Gerudos Valley stand open to all who answer the call of the Horde.

Whether you're a seasoned warrior, an aspiring champion, or a wandering adventurer seeking a place to call home, there is a place for you within our walls.

We ask for no minimum playtime, no mandatory battles, and no endless grind to earn your place among us. But when you pledge yourself to a raid or warband, your fellow warriors are counting on you. Commitment is a choice. Honor that choice.

The Heart of the Valley

The People of Gerudos Valley

Not every hero wears a crown, commands an army, or marches into battle every night.

Some guard our walls. Some gather supplies. Others explore distant lands, lend a helping hand, or simply enjoy the warmth of good company. Every one of them makes the Valley stronger.

These are our current Discord role names and the kinds of contributions they represent. Competitive team membership and officer appointments are separate from ordinary membership. Optional interest roles never imply required attendance.

A Place for Every Adventurer

Wayfarers are our general guild members; Valley Guests are welcome in the public social community. PvP Interest, Raid Interest, Dungeon Interest and Community Events are optional choices. PvP Core and Raid Core are separate, voluntarily accepted commitments.

You don't need to raid, PvP, lead events, or maintain a particular schedule to call Gerudos Valley your home.

Join when you can. Fight when you wish. Help when you're able. When you make a commitment, honor it.

Toward the Dragon's Lair

We Intend to Raid as Far as We're Allowed

Gerudos Valley is not only a PvP guild. We also intend to build a serious raid core and push progression through every stage of endgame content available to us on WoW Forever.

That means showing up prepared, growing a dependable core, and continuing our march through the content our server allows. Friday remains our focused progression night, while Sunday gives us room for open runs, makeups, alternates, and building experience across the roster.

Our pixel-art vision of a Horde raid gathering before Onyxia captures the spirit of the journey: preparation, courage and the teammates standing beside us.
