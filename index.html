Our promise

Anyone can belong. Commitment is optional. Reliability matters when you sign up.

No minimum playtime, mandatory raids, or pressure to maintain rank just to stay in the guild. Our organized teams have their own signup and preparation expectations. If you're confirmed for an event, others are counting on you.

The weekly rhythm · tentative

Weekday PvP

Rank-progress groups and, as we grow, tactical battleground premades.

Wednesday

No official guild events. Rest day.

Friday

Focused core progression. Leveling and dungeons while the roster forms.

Saturday

Horde War Night. Accessible world PvP and good company.

Sunday

Optional open, alternate, or pickup raid activities where lockouts permit.

Exact event times live in the in-game calendar. Discord has the conversation.

What comes later?

Guild events, friendly competitions, occasional free-entry giveaways after rules review, and perhaps more online tools if the community actually needs them. For now, we're keeping it simple and getting the company together.
